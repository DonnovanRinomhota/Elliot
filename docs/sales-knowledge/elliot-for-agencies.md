## What Elliot is

Elliot is an AI front office for estate agencies. It answers enquiries from
buyers and renters on the agency's website chat and email (WhatsApp is in
testing, see below), day and night, asks them the right questions, shows matching listings with links and
photos, and books viewings straight into the agency's Google Calendar. The
agency's team sees every conversation in the Elliot dashboard and can take
over at any time.

Elliot is made and run by Donnovan Rinomhota in Poland. Agencies deal with him
directly: he sets Elliot up, watches how it performs and adjusts it with them.

## What it does today

- **Answers enquiries in seconds**, at any hour, from the agency's own
  listings, fees and FAQs. If it doesn't know, it says so and passes the
  question to the team.
- **Shows real listings**: searches the agency's listings by area, budget,
  size, rooms and buy or rent, and sends each with a link to its photos and
  details. If nothing matches exactly, it suggests the closest options and says
  how they differ.
- **Qualifies buyers and renters**: budget, area, size, timing and financing,
  and marks each lead hot, warm or cold so the team knows who to call first.
- **Books viewings** in free slots of the agents' own Google Calendars (the
  client's own agent first), and emails the client a confirmation in English
  and Polish. The
  agency sets the earliest booking time and a gap between viewings for travel.
- **Reminds clients the day before** (by email; on WhatsApp once WhatsApp is live). Clients can reply
  to confirm, move or cancel; Elliot does it, updates the calendar and tells
  the agent.
- **Wins sellers and landlords**: when someone wants to sell or let their
  property, or asks what it is worth, Elliot never guesses a price. It asks
  about the property, marks them as a seller (hot if they want to sell within
  3 months), alerts an agent and books a free valuation visit at their address.
- **Alerts the team immediately** when a client asks for a person, wants
  something only a person can do, or is a hot lead, with a summary and the
  full conversation.
- **Captures every lead** in the dashboard and, if the agency uses one, sends
  it on to their CRM (directly or through Zapier or Make).
- **Follows up** with people who go quiet, by email (WhatsApp is in
  testing), with the agency's approval rules. A client who answers STOP gets
  no more follow-ups.
- **Reads what clients send by email** (in testing): PDFs and photos (a mortgage
  pre-approval, a floor plan, a photo of a flat they liked) are read and used
  in the reply; other files are kept for the team. All files are shown in the
  dashboard.
- **Daily summary** email for the team each morning.

## Listings

The agency's listings are typed in or imported from a spreadsheet, PDF or
Word file. Copying them from **ASARI CRM** automatically (every hour) is in
testing.
Each listing gets a page with its photos that Elliot can link to.

## Channels

- **Website chat**: a chat window added to the agency's website with one line
  of code.
- **WhatsApp Business** (in testing): the same conversations on the
  agency's WhatsApp number, with instant replies.
- **Email**: the agency forwards its inbox (Gmail, Outlook or any other) to
  its Elliot address; Elliot replies to enquiries in the same email thread,
  in the agency's name, and clients' replies go back to the agency's inbox.
  Portal enquiries (Otodom, OLX) are answered too. Confirmations and reminders
  are sent in the agency's name as well, optionally from its own domain.
- **Phone calls**: not offered yet.

## Languages

Elliot replies in the language the client writes in. Polish and English are
used every day; other languages such as Ukrainian, German or Russian work too.

## The dashboard

Every conversation, lead, viewing and follow-up in one place: conversations
on all channels, leads by status, a calendar of viewings per agent, listings
with photos, escalations, approvals and team members with roles. Team members
can join any chat and reply themselves.

## Control

The agency decides what Elliot may do on its own and what needs approval:
follow-ups can wait for approval, and so can viewings if the agency prefers
to confirm each one itself.
Clients can ask for a person at any time. Everything is recorded, and the
agency's own information (listings, prices, policies) is what Elliot answers
from; changes apply straight away.

## Works with

Google Calendar (each agent's own), any email inbox (Gmail, Outlook /
Microsoft 365, or the agency's own mail server, by forwarding), the agency's
website, and CRMs through a webhook, Zapier or Make. In testing: WhatsApp
Business and ASARI CRM (listings). Not yet: Outlook calendars. For another CRM such as
Galactica Virgo or IMO, book a call to discuss the connection.

## Data and privacy

Elliot works for the agency: the agency decides what it says and owns its
data. Each agency's data is kept separate from every other agency's. Personal
data is handled under the GDPR. The Elliot team can open an agency's dashboard
only while the agency allows it (Settings → Elliot support access, for 24 hours,
7 days or during set-up), and the agency sees every part they opened. Clients are told at the start that they are talking to an
AI assistant, and can ask for a person at any time (as the EU AI Act requires).
A data processing agreement (umowa powierzenia) is signed with every agency. For contract terms and data processing
details, book a call with Donnovan.
